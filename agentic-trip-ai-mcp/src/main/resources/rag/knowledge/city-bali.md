## Stable destination knowledge

Bali is a Indonesian island and major tourism destination. This document contains durable destination context for RAG;
it is not a source of live operational information.

### Core attractions and areas
- Ubud
- Tanah Lot
- Uluwatu Temple
- Tegallalang rice terraces
- Nusa Dua

### Culture and heritage
Balinese Hindu traditions, temple ceremonies, rice landscapes, arts and beach destinations are central to the island's identity.

### Food and local experiences
- nasi campur
- satay
- lawar and Balinese dishes
