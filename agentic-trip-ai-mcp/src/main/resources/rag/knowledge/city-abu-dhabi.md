## Stable destination knowledge

Abu Dhabi is a capital of the United Arab Emirates. This document contains durable destination context for RAG;
it is not a source of live operational information.

### Core attractions and areas
- Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque
- Louvre Abu Dhabi
- Qasr Al Watan
- Corniche waterfront
- Saadiyat Island

### Culture and heritage
Modern Gulf capital with strong Islamic architectural heritage, museums, waterfront public spaces and family-oriented cultural attractions.

### Food and local experiences
- Arabic and Emirati cuisine
- dates and Arabic coffee
- machboos and grilled dishes
