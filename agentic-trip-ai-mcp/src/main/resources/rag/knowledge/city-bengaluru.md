## Stable destination knowledge

Bengaluru is a major technology and cultural city in Karnataka, India. This document contains durable destination context for RAG;
it is not a source of live operational information.

- Bengaluru Palace
- Lalbagh
- Cubbon Park
- Vidhana Soudha
- KR Market

### Culture and heritage
Known for technology, pleasant urban green spaces, Kannada culture, historic institutions and a diverse modern food scene.

### Food and local experiences

- filter coffee
- Kannadiga and multi-cuisine food
