## Stable destination knowledge

Cape Town is a coastal city in South Africa. This document contains durable destination context for RAG;
it is not a source of live operational information.

### Core attractions and areas
- Table Mountain
- V&A Waterfront
- Robben Island
- Bo-Kaap
- Cape Peninsula

### Culture and heritage
Cape Malay, African and European influences meet dramatic coastal and mountain landscapes.

### Food and local experiences
- Cape Malay cuisine
- seafood
- braai and local wines
