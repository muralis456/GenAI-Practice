## Stable destination knowledge

Kochi is a historic port city in Kerala, India. This document contains durable destination context for RAG;
it is not a source of live operational information.

### Core attractions and areas
- Fort Kochi
- Chinese fishing nets
- Mattancherry Palace
- Jewish Town
- Marine Drive

### Culture and heritage
Kerala coastal culture reflects Indian Ocean trade, Jewish, Arab, Chinese, Portuguese and Dutch historical influences.

### Food and local experiences
- appam and stew
- Kerala seafood
- puttu and kadala
