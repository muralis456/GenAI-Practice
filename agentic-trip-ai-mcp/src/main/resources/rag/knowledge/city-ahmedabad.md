## Stable destination knowledge

Ahmedabad is a major city in Gujarat, India. This document contains durable destination context for RAG;
it is not a source of live operational information.

### Core attractions and areas
- Sabarmati Ashram
- Adalaj Stepwell
- Sidi Saiyyed Mosque
- old-city pols
- Sabarmati Riverfront

### Culture and heritage
Historic Gujarati city known for textile heritage, pol houses, stepwells, Jain and Hindu traditions and a strong food culture.

### Food and local experiences
- Gujarati thali
- fafda-jalebi
- khakhra and local snacks
