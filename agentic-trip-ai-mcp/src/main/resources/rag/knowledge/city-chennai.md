## Stable destination knowledge

Chennai is a capital of Tamil Nadu, India. This document contains durable destination context for RAG;
it is not a source of live operational information.

### Core attractions and areas
- Marina Beach
- Kapaleeshwarar Temple
- Fort St George
- Government Museum
- Mylapore

### Culture and heritage
Tamil culture, Dravidian temple traditions, Carnatic music and a long coastal history are central to Chennai.

### Food and local experiences
- idli and dosa
- filter coffee
- Chettinad and Tamil cuisine
