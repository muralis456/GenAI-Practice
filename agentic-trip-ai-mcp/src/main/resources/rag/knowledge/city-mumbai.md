Mumbai is a major financial and cultural city on India's west coast. This document contains durable destination context for RAG;
it is not a source of live operational information.

- Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus
- Marine Drive
- Elephanta Caves

Marathi culture, colonial architecture, cinema, finance and dense coastal neighborhoods shape Mumbai.
