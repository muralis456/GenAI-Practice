## Stable destination knowledge

Goa is a coastal state and tourism destination in India. This document contains durable destination context for RAG;
it is not a source of live operational information.

### Core attractions and areas
- Old Goa churches
- Panaji
- beaches
- Fort Aguada
- spice and village landscapes

### Culture and heritage
Goan identity blends Konkani traditions with Portuguese-era heritage, Catholic and Hindu cultural influences and coastal leisure.

### Food and local experiences
- Goan fish curry
- vindaloo
- bebinca
